# Privacy Policy for Akmonis

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Akmonis is built on a **privacy-first, local-playback philosophy**. We design our software so you can enjoy your music library locally, without being monetized or spied on.

---

### 1. Local-First & Audio Privacy
* **Your Files Stay Local:** Your audio files, track names, playlist content, and playback history remain strictly on your local device. We never scan, upload, or monetize your local library.
* **No Ad Tracking:** We do not sell data, profile your personal identity, or use advertising networks.

### 2. Anonymous Diagnostics & Usage Analytics
To understand which features are helpful, identify performance bottlenecks, and fix bugs, Akmonis may collect **strictly anonymous, aggregated operational data**, such as:
* App crashes, error logs, and basic hardware parameters (e.g., OS build, audio driver crash codes).
* Aggregated feature interaction metrics (e.g., whether a specific DSP module or AutoEQ profile was toggled).

*What we never collect:*
* No personal identities (PII), IP addresses stored with your profile, or device identifiers.
* No filenames, metadata of your personal songs, or listening habits.

### 3. Network Activity (On-Demand Features)
Audio playback, DSP processing, and AutoEQ calibration operate **100% offline**. Akmonis only connects to the internet when you explicitly invoke specific online features:
* **Lyrics & LLM Translation:** Track titles or lyric text are sent to online lyric providers or the designated LLM API solely to fetch and translate lyrics upon request.
* **Artwork & Metadata:** When retrieving missing artwork or track information, the app queries public databases using basic identifiers (such as artist, album name, or file checksums). No personal audio files or user identities are ever uploaded.

### 4. API Keys & Credentials
If you supply your own API keys (e.g., for LLM translation services), they are stored locally on your machine and only transmitted directly to the designated endpoint.
